#+TITLE: 023 — start only: a person says change:start and the process takes over
#+DATE: 2026-09-14

* Hypothesis

Five agents each open one single-line PR and do exactly one further thing:
apply =change:start=. Everything after that is the process: a scheduler on
the mini (=change/scheduler.sh=) that runs =change/driver.sh= one change at
a time, and the forge's workflows. The hypothesis: *a change whose owner
says only start reaches production and settles, and every refusal it meets
is stated on the PR in words its owner can act on without knowing the
pipeline.* Falsified if an owner has to do anything but rebase and say start
again, or if a ticket ends in a state its owner cannot read.

Restarts =experiments/022=, where the same five steps were driven by hand
and the owner's finding was that the tickets were dirty: five tickets
(#97–#101, the other operator's) carried verdicts of a release that never
ended. In this run the driver treats a start on a dirty ticket as paying
that debt (clears the previous release's markers, says so, keeps the
person's own words) rather than asking a person to sweep.

* Setup

- Estate: block 1 (=:9010=) as staging and block 2 (=:9020=) as production,
  redeployed by the driver to the head under release. No windows booked.
- Intent label: =change:start= (label-owners.tsv: supersedes
  =change:requested= + =release=). The scheduler runs the oldest PR number
  first; the label carries no time, so who said start first is not on the
  list.
- Guard 0, first version: the driver refuses a head behind =main= and
  clears every marker including the intent; the owner rebases and says
  start again. Replaced at 23:56Z (see Findings, F1) by the forge's own
  "update branch": a merge of =main= into the branch, nothing of the
  person's rewritten; the driver refuses only a conflict.
- The second identity approves under its standing delegation; the review
  says so. The clearing at end has no identity of its own: it is whoever's
  =gh= is active on the host (jwalsh here, aygp-dr on hydra, the actions bot
  in a workflow). See §Findings.
- Unplanned again: the other operator is live on #97–#101 against the same
  label lock (scenarios D21).
- Logs: =scheduler.log= (the driver's own account), =label-timeline.log=
  (a 20 s poll of every label change on the five PRs).

* Findings

** F1 — refuse-and-rebase livelocks against a moving trunk (23:50–23:54Z)

All five PRs (#104–#108) were refused at guard 0 on their first start: they
had branched from =main= seconds before the driver commit landed. Two
owners rebased and said start again within three minutes, and were refused
again: the other operator landed #101 at 23:49:51 and #99 at 23:53:49, so
=main= moved between the rebase and the driver's look, twice. With a trunk
moving every four minutes and a person's rebase, push, labeller wait and
second start taking three, "rebase and say start again" never converges.
This is the merge queue's reason for existing (#102) arriving as an
incident. Fix taken: the driver asks the forge to bring the branch up to
=main= (=gh pr update-branch=, a merge commit authored by the forge), waits
for the new head and the labeller, and continues; it says so on the PR.
A person is asked only for a conflict. Guard 0 also now runs before the
lock: the first version held the berth for fifteen seconds to discover a
stale head (#104, #105).

** F2 — the tickets were dirty because five releases never ended

Reported as #103. The other operator's loop re-runs the staging
instruments on #97–#101 every ~8 minutes and never ends the release, so
each carries =staging:e2e staging:smoke staging:uat= and the retired
=staging:failed=; five tickets accepted at staging at once is not a state
the estate can be in. The driver's start-sweep makes a start on such a
ticket pay the missing =change:end=; the loop should have paid it.

** F3 — the reaper has no identity

The clearing at end (=settle.sh=, =abort.sh=, =reap.sh=) runs as whichever
=gh= account is active on the host: =jwalsh= here, =aygp-dr= on hydra,
=github-actions[bot]= in a workflow. A machine's removal is indistinguishable
on the forge from a person's, which is D21's finding from the other side.
