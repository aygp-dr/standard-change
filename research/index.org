This document is the research directory of the repository, assembled by
=#+INCLUDE= from =history/=, =substrates/=, =findings/= here and =experiments/= so it can be
rebuilt as HTML or PDF (=gmake research=). It is in the order a rebuilder needs: how to adopt it, what was found,
the experiments, where it can run, then the history of the ideas as an
appendix, with the tables the paper cites last. It leaves out the internals: the spec, the scripts,
the gates, the shell review. A repository with a risk designation, something
deployable and a pipeline that governs deployment can take the tracking layer
described in Part I without touching the labels it already has.
