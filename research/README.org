#+TITLE: research/

The history and the research, none of the internals, as one document.

- =index.org=: the master; =#+INCLUDE= of =docs/= and =experiments/= plus =adoption.org=.
- =adoption.org=: how a repository with its own risk designation and deployable labels takes the tracking layer without renaming anything, and every decision with its options.
- =build/=: =gmake= here (or =gmake build= at the root) writes =index.html= and =index.pdf=; =gmake html= or =gmake pdf= for one; git-ignored.
- =export-common.el=, =export-html.el=, =export-pdf.el=: the batch export; the common hook strips the included files' own title keywords so the master's wins.

The included files are not copied; edit them where they live. Adding a
document is one =#+INCLUDE= line under the right part.
