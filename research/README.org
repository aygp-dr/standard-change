#+TITLE: research/

The history and the research, none of the internals, as one document.

- =index.org=: the master; =#+INCLUDE= of =history/=, =substrates/=, =findings/= and =../experiments/*/notes.org=, plus =adoption.org=.
- =history/=: what was read (timelines, surveys, references). =substrates/=: where it could run and what a platform can enforce. =findings/=: what this repository found. Internals (spec, scripts, gates, ADRs, the shell review) stay in =docs/= and are not included.
- =adoption.org=: how a repository with its own risk designation and deployable labels takes the tracking layer without renaming anything, and every decision with its options.
- =build/=: =gmake= here (or =gmake build= at the root) writes =index.html= and =index.pdf=; =gmake html= or =gmake pdf= for one; git-ignored.
- =puppeteer.json=: the browser mermaid-cli renders with (this machine's desktop Chrome; edit the path elsewhere). A block mmdc cannot render stays verbatim and the export log says so.
- =export-common.el=, =export-html.el=, =export-pdf.el=: the batch export; Mermaid blocks become figures via mmdc; the common hook strips the included files' own title keywords so the master's wins.

The included files are not copied; edit them where they live. Adding a
document is one =#+INCLUDE= line under the right part.
