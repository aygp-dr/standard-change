#+TITLE: Glossary: one word, several domains, and the words that moved here
#+DATE: 2026-09-16

The spec's nomenclature (Part I) defines this repository's terms of art.
This glossary does two other things. The first table is for the reader
arriving from another domain: the same word means something else in git,
in release engineering, on a platform, and in ITIL, and the paper uses it
in one sense. The second table is for the reader of the history: the words
that changed meaning inside this project between the 12th and the 16th,
and why. A term absent from both is used in its ordinary sense.

* Words that moved inside this project

| word              | first meaning (09-12 to 09-13)                              | later meaning (09-14 to 09-16)                                                  | why it moved                                                                       |
|-------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| the person's start | =release= then =change:requested=, two labels for one fact  | =change:start=, then =release:start=: one verb, consumed by the runner           | two human-owned labels for one fact (README); the tense-pair grammar (#125)         |
| hold              | =hold:staging=, rejected as a third stop with no rule       | =staging:hold= at one scope, then =release:hold=: the person's word, environment-free | the scope rule, then "the words name the intent, never the environment"      |
| berth             | a per-group staging slot                                     | the singleton =deploy:staging= label, then a lock record with a holder           | scenario D4; D21                                                                   |
| deployed          | =deployed:staging-<group>-<n>=, a claim written by the deployer | =staging:deployed= (install finished) beside =staging:healthy= (observed serving) | a label with no build attached authorizes nothing (014)                          |
| passed            | =staging:passed=, one verdict                                | =staging:e2e=, =staging:smoke=, =staging:uat=, one per instrument, each naming the build | one writer per fact (label-ownership rule 1)                                |
| complete, end     | =change:complete= the outcome; =change:end= a persistent tombstone | =release:completed= the outcome before the merge; =release:ended= the runner's answer to end, kept only when there is no =mergedAt= | the merge is the record (#122); one namespace, two tenses (#125) |
| unaffected        | =release:unaffected=, an adjective the person says            | =release:skip=, a verb like the others; the runner answers =release:skipped=      | the person's words are verbs                                                        |
| scheduled         | =change:scheduled=, the pipeline's record of a booking        | =release:scheduled=, still the runner's; =release:schedule= is the person's ask   | the port (experiment 025)                                                          |
| environment       | staging 9201, production 9200                                | staging 9200, blue 9210, green 9220, front 9230; dev blocks =9000 + 10n=         | the port map corrected on the 13th; the number says which tier                     |
| estate            | "the jails on hydra"                                          | the third subject beside classification and lifecycle, on something that cannot be deployed | spec 0.5; the freeze and the emergency needed a home that no change carries |
| runner            | a GitHub Actions runner, which cannot see the estate           | the host-side driver and scheduler that answer the person                       | merge-on-healthy refused from ubuntu-latest; 023                                    |
| verified          | the README's lifecycle state after e2e and smoke               | the auditor's verdict on a citation (=:VERDICT: correct=)                       | two vocabularies met in the research writeup; the drawer is the record             |
