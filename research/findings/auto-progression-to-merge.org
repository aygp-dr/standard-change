* The two gates, named

A change that says =release:start= crosses exactly two gates before the
forge's own merge record becomes the account of what happened. Both are
re-derived at the moment they matter, not trusted from an earlier point in
time -- the repo's own recurring lesson (=research/findings/concurrent-change.org=,
kind 4: "an observation outlives the build it described") is why neither
gate reads a label and stops there.

** Gate 1 -- promotion to production (staging -> production)

Fires on a =labeled= event, when the label added is =staging:smoke= or
=staging:uat= (.github/workflows/promote.yml:14, the =if:= condition --
promotion fires on whichever of the two lands last, since 2026-09-16, #60).

Three checks, in order, each one capable of stopping everything after it:

1. */Gates green on the head SHA, re-derived, not assumed./* Reads
   =check-runs= for that exact SHA and takes the *latest run per gate name*,
   because every label event re-triggers =gate.yml= and an in-progress
   duplicate must read as "not yet," not as "red" -- promotion failed on #93
   for exactly the opposite mistake, four seconds after =staging:smoke=
   landed (promote.yml:20-33). =gate-selftest= must itself appear in that
   set, or the gate results it would have validated are void
   (promote.yml:32).

2. */A person's =staging:hold= stops this, here./* Not earlier, not
   implicitly -- checked at the moment of promoting, nowhere else
   (promote.yml:39-47). This is the *only* place =staging:hold= is ever
   checked in the whole pipeline.

3. */All three staging observations, on this head./* =staging:e2e=,
   =staging:smoke=, =staging:uat= -- the third is a person's acceptance and
   cannot be re-derived by any script, which is exactly why the run that
   lands =deploy:production= is the one where the *last* of the three
   arrives (promote.yml:49-, the =req= step).

If all three pass, the job lands =deploy:production= itself; nothing beyond
that workflow makes the actual production deployment happen. That is a
separate consumer of the label, out of scope for this note.

** staging:hold, in full -- because nobody else writes this down

=staging:hold= is *exclusively a person's label.* Grep the whole =change/=
tree and no script ever calls
=gh pr edit ... --add-label staging:hold=. =change/driver.sh:88= says so in
a comment: "the person's own words (=staging:hold=, =release:backfill-owed=)."
It exists at level 2 of the compliance ladder (=README.org=, "Levels of
compliance": "=release:hold=, one person-owned pause" -- corrected
2026-09-20 in the spec-sealing pass to read =staging:hold=, the label that
actually exists).

*Clearing it is where the gap is.* No script ever *deliberately* removes
=staging:hold= either. It appears in exactly two label-clearing sweeps:

- =change/abort.sh:108= -- cleared along with every other working label when
  a change is aborted.
- =change/queue.sh:60= -- cleared when a change is dequeued.

Both are *incidental*: =staging:hold= is one name among a dozen being wiped
because the change record itself is closing, not because anyone decided the
hold specifically should lift. There is no =change/unhold.sh=, no skill
equivalent to =release-start='s, no command whose job is "a person now says
proceed." The only way to lift a hold on a change that is *not* being
aborted or dequeued is a bare =gh pr edit --remove-label staging:hold=,
typed by hand, undocumented anywhere as the sanctioned way to do it. Compare
=change/driver.sh=, which treats =staging:hold= as one of a small set of
labels a person may hold *at all* (driver.sh:93, the case arm that leaves
=release:start=, =staging:hold=, =release:backfill-owed=, =blocked:lock=,
=app:*=, =itil:*=, =control-plane= alone rather than treating them as the
driver's own state) -- the write side is specified; the *un*-write side
never was.

** Gate 2 -- settlement (production -> the merge)

=change/settle.sh <pr>= is the terminal transition, and its own header
comment states the design precisely: the terminal state is
=release:completed=, not =production:healthy= and not =staging:uat=,
because the first is a statement about *the change record* and the other
two are statements about *a build* -- and observations about a build expire
when the head moves; a change record does not, "because it is a statement
about something that happened rather than about something that is currently
true" (settle.sh:4-14).

Order of operations, each one capable of stopping settlement outright:

1. */Production is asked what it is serving, right now./* Not "was healthy
   once" -- an HTTP probe against the live front door, compared against the
   PR's current head SHA (settle.sh:38-42). This is guard 5, re-derived
   directly rather than trusted from a stale label -- the file's own
   rationale (settle.sh:44-67) is worth reading in full: requiring the label
   *in addition to* the live probe once let a workflow that cannot reach
   production veto a real settlement by withdrawing a measurement it never
   had standing to take, three times, on #9.
2. */e2e and smoke, re-derived against the current head; =staging:uat=,
   required as a label./* The first two are re-checked the same way guard 4
   checks review records, because a second operator's label sweep once
   withdrew every label from #92 *after* production had gone healthy and
   guard 4 had authorized it -- and =settle.sh= read the labels instead of
   the record, and refused a change that had, in fact, settled
   (settle.sh:69-83). =staging:uat= alone stays label-gated: a person's
   acceptance cannot be re-run by any script, so a person's withdrawal of it
   must stand (settle.sh:78-79).
3. */Guard 3, reported, not enforced, here./* Was the deployment inside a
   booked window? Refusing at settlement would leave production ahead of
   trunk, which is worse than the deviation it would be objecting to -- so
   this step records a deviation in the PIR rather than blocking
   (settle.sh:98-117).
4. */=mergeStateStatus= re-checked, because trunk can move between the
   promotion workflow and this script./* =BEHIND= or =DIRTY= stops
   settlement and marks the change =blocked:diverged= (settle.sh:135-141).
5. */=release:completed= is written -- before the merge, not after./*
   "Set before the merge, because the merge is what follows completion, not
   what constitutes it" (settle.sh:143-144). This is the fact the user
   asked about: the participle the runner auto-applies once everything
   above has passed, and it is applied *before* =gh pr merge= runs, not as
   a confirmation afterward.
6. */The merge./* =gh pr merge --squash --delete-branch=, and a failed
   merge call is treated as fatal to the rest of settlement -- checked
   directly, not just attempted and assumed -- because a prior version of
   this script said "merged" for a PR that was still open and conflicting,
   observed for real on #40 (settle.sh:161-177, the comment names the
   incident in full).
7. */The PIR is posted, then the working labels are cleared, then
   =release:completed= itself is cleared and =release:ended= written./*
   The record (the merge commit, =mergedAt=, the PIR comment) outlives the
   labels on purpose -- see =README.org=, "The merge is the tombstone": on
   the merged path the tombstone is =mergedAt=, never a label.

* The whole shape, one table

| step | what's asked | where | stops everything after it on failure |
|---+---+---+---|
| 1 | gates green on this exact head SHA | promote.yml | yes |
| 2 | staging:hold absent | promote.yml | yes, exit 78 |
| 3 | staging:e2e, staging:smoke, staging:uat all on this head | promote.yml | yes (waits for the last one) |
| -- | =deploy:production= lands; a separate consumer deploys | -- | -- |
| 4 | production observed serving this head, asked now | settle.sh | yes |
| 5 | e2e/smoke re-derived on this head; staging:uat present as a label | settle.sh | yes |
| 6 | deployed inside a booked window | settle.sh | no -- recorded as a deviation in the PIR |
| 7 | mergeStateStatus is clean (not BEHIND/DIRTY) | settle.sh | yes, marks blocked:diverged |
| 8 | release:completed written | settle.sh | (this is the fact itself, not a check) |
| 9 | gh pr merge --squash --delete-branch | settle.sh | yes, exits 8, settlement stops |
| 10 | PIR posted, labels cleared, release:ended written | settle.sh | -- terminal |

* Reference implementation (tangle target, illustrative only)

Mirrors steps 1-3 and 4-9 above as two standalone scripts. These are a
*teaching mirror*, trimmed of the incident-specific hardening comments and
the live HTTP probes the real scripts use -- an agent wanting the actual,
battle-tested version should read =change/settle.sh= and
=.github/workflows/promote.yml= directly, cited throughout this file by line
number. Tangling this file writes these two files under =reference/=; it
does not touch =change/= or =.github/workflows/=.

#+begin_src sh :tangle reference/auto-progression/gate1-promote.sh :shebang "#!/bin/sh" :mkdirp t
# gate1-promote.sh <pr> -- illustrative mirror of .github/workflows/promote.yml
# Real thing: fires on a `labeled` event for staging:smoke or staging:uat.
# This mirror takes the PR number and re-derives the same three checks.
set -eu
pr="${1:?usage: gate1-promote.sh <pr>}"
R="${GH_REPO:-${GITHUB_REPOSITORY:?set GH_REPO or GITHUB_REPOSITORY}}"

sha=$(gh pr view "$pr" --repo "$R" --json headRefOid -q .headRefOid)
labels=$(gh pr view "$pr" --repo "$R" --json labels -q '[.labels[].name]|join(" ")')

# 1. gates green on this exact head -- real version waits up to 10 minutes
#    for in-progress runs and takes latest-per-gate-name. This mirror does
#    one check, no retry loop -- see promote.yml:20-33 for the real one.
bad=$(gh api "repos/$R/commits/$sha/check-runs?per_page=100" \
        --jq '[.check_runs[] | select(.conclusion != "success")] | length')
[ "$bad" -eq 0 ] || { echo "gate(s) not green on $sha" >&2; exit 1; }

# 2. staging:hold stops this here, and only here.
case " $labels " in
  *" staging:hold "*) echo "staging:hold is on #$pr -- not proceeding" >&2; exit 78 ;;
esac

# 3. all three staging observations, on this head (label presence only --
#    real version re-derives e2e/smoke against the head SHA via evidence.sh).
for req in staging:e2e staging:smoke staging:uat; do
  case " $labels " in
    *" $req "*) ;;
    *) echo "not yet: $req not recorded" >&2; exit 0 ;;
  esac
done

gh pr edit "$pr" --repo "$R" --add-label deploy:production >/dev/null
echo "deploy:production landed on #$pr @ $(echo "$sha" | cut -c1-7)"
#+end_src

#+begin_src sh :tangle reference/auto-progression/gate2-settle.sh :shebang "#!/bin/sh" :mkdirp t
# gate2-settle.sh <pr> -- illustrative mirror of change/settle.sh
# Real thing probes a live front door for X-Build-Sha; this mirror takes
# "what production is serving" as an argument instead, so it can run without
# a real estate. See change/settle.sh for the actual HTTP-probe version.
set -eu
pr="${1:?usage: gate2-settle.sh <pr> <production-sha>}"
served="${2:?usage: gate2-settle.sh <pr> <production-sha>}"
R="${GH_REPO:-${GITHUB_REPOSITORY:?set GH_REPO or GITHUB_REPOSITORY}}"

sha=$(gh pr view "$pr" --repo "$R" --json headRefOid -q .headRefOid)
short=$(echo "$sha" | cut -c1-7)
labels=$(gh pr view "$pr" --repo "$R" --json labels -q '[.labels[].name]|join(" ")')

# 4. production observed serving this head, asked now (caller supplies the
#    observation; the real script asks a live host itself).
[ "$served" = "$short" ] || { echo "production serves '$served', not $short" >&2; exit 1; }

# 5. staging:uat required as a label (e2e/smoke re-derivation omitted in
#    this mirror -- see change/evidence.sh and settle.sh:69-83 for the real
#    record-over-label logic).
case " $labels " in
  *" staging:uat "*) ;;
  *) echo "staging:uat missing -- no person's acceptance on this head" >&2; exit 1 ;;
esac

# 7. mergeStateStatus must be clean.
ms=$(gh pr view "$pr" --repo "$R" --json mergeStateStatus -q .mergeStateStatus)
case "$ms" in
  BEHIND|DIRTY) echo "main moved during deploy ($ms)" >&2; exit 9 ;;
esac

# 8. release:completed, before the merge.
gh pr edit "$pr" --repo "$R" --add-label release:completed >/dev/null
echo "release:completed on #$pr -- merging"

# 9. the merge, checked, not assumed.
if gh pr merge "$pr" --repo "$R" --squash --delete-branch >/dev/null 2>&1; then
  echo "merged, branch deleted"
else
  echo "REFUSED: merge failed, settlement stops here" >&2
  exit 8
fi
#+end_src

* Tested in a minimal context [E]

Done, 2026-09-21, against a real disposable scratch repo
(=jwalsh/auto-progression-test=, private, archived after), because every
incident cited above (#9, #40, #92, #93, #60) is evidence about individual
guards under production load, not evidence that the *whole* chain -- gate 1
through gate 2 through an actual merge -- had ever been run against a clean,
minimal target on purpose.

Setup: the seven labels above, a trivial =gate-selftest= workflow that
always succeeds (real check-runs, not faked ones -- =gate1-promote.sh='s
check-run query needs a genuine =check-runs= API response to test
honestly), one branch, one real PR.

Result: every step behaved exactly as documented above, observed directly
rather than inferred from the source:

1. =release:start= alone, gates green, no staging evidence -- "not yet:
   staging:e2e not recorded," exit 0. Correctly does not proceed.
2. All three staging labels added, plus =staging:hold= -- "staging:hold is
   on #1 -- not proceeding," *exit 78*, no =deploy:production=. The hold
   overrides everything else, exactly as designed.
3. =staging:hold= removed by hand -- there being no other way, confirmed by
   trying to find one and finding none -- =gate1= immediately lands
   =deploy:production=.
4. =gate2-settle.sh 1 <head-sha>= (the local head, standing in for a real
   production probe reporting what it serves): =release:completed= applied,
   then =gh pr merge --squash --delete-branch= ran for real. The PR's final
   state: =MERGED=, a real =mergedAt= timestamp, =release:completed= present
   alongside the rest of the trail.

*One honest limitation surfaced by building the test, not by running it.*
=gate1-promote.sh='s check-run gate is =bad=$(... | length); [ "$bad" -eq 0
]= -- if the query returns *zero* check-runs (no CI ever ran on this SHA at
all), =bad= is trivially =0= and the gate vacuously passes. This test never
exercised that path, because it deliberately stood up a real, passing
=gate-selftest= workflow first. The real =promote.yml= does not have this
gap: it explicitly requires the name =gate-selftest= to appear among the
completed runs (promote.yml:32), which the mirror above dropped for brevity.
Worth fixing in the mirror if it is ever used for more than illustration --
left as-is here so the diff between "the real thing" and "the simplified
teaching version" stays visible rather than quietly patched away.
